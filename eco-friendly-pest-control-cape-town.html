Trusted local pest control for Cape Town

Conventional pesticides can leave unwanted residues in the rooms where your family lives, plays, and sleeps. GreenLeaf provides eco-friendly pest control in Cape Town with a careful, targeted approach: we identify the pest, its harbourage points, and the least disruptive treatment that can solve the problem.

Our child- and pet-conscious service options include gel-bait cockroach control, humane rodent management, colony-targeted ant treatments, and low-residue whole-house fumigation. Every treatment plan includes clear preparation and re-entry guidance, so you know how to protect people, pets, and food during application.

Questions about safety?

When can we re-enter after treatment?

Most treatments are safe to re-enter after 2–4 hours; your technician provides product-specific guidance.

Are products safe for pets?

We use SANS-approved options and ask that pets stay away only while treatment is applied and dried.
